Treasury futures duration profile, 2026-11-03

ZNZ6, TNZ6, ZBZ6, UBZ6. Futures DV01 and key-rate DV01s per $100mm notional, probability-weighted over the deliverable basket with a normal distribution of the CTD's forward yield (at-the-money implied vol), against the CTD-based figures most systems report. Shifts move every basket forward yield in parallel; percentiles are of the move to the implied delivery date.

Detailed analytics for each contract (the shifted states, key-rate DV01s, CTD probabilities, the distance to each switch, and the inputs) are on its own page: follow the ticker links in the tables below. ZT (2-year) and ZF (5-year) futures are omitted: their deliverable baskets span about a year of maturity or less, so a change of CTD moves their duration and DV01 little.

2 Implied delivery: the last delivery day if the basket's carry against term repo, weighted by each bond's option-implied probability of being CTD, is positive; the first if negative. One date for every bond and scenario: where bonds of opposite carry are close (ZN's Aug-33s), the date is uncertain and could be either.

Duration: the DV01 over the futures settlement price, in years (the futures' modified duration).
