Treasury futures duration profile, 2026-10-23

ZNZ6, TNZ6, ZBZ6, UBZ6. Futures DV01 and key-rate DV01s per $100mm notional, probability-weighted over the deliverable basket with a normal distribution of the CTD's forward yield (at-the-money implied vol), against the CTD-based figures most systems report. Shifts move every basket forward yield in parallel; percentiles are of the move to the implied delivery date.

Detailed analytics for each contract (the shifted states, key-rate DV01s, CTD probabilities, the distance to each switch, and the inputs) are on its own page: follow the ticker links in the table below. ZT (2-year) and ZF (5-year) futures are omitted: their deliverable baskets span about a year of maturity or less, so a change of CTD moves their duration and DV01 little.

1 P(RCaD): the probability that today's CTD remains CTD at the implied delivery date. O: from the option digitals, which can only measure it at option expiry (20 Nov 2026); N: the ATM normal factor to the implied delivery date, as in the charts.

2 Implied delivery: the last delivery day if the basket's carry against term repo, weighted by each bond's option-implied probability of being CTD, is positive; the first if negative. One date for every bond and scenario: where bonds of opposite carry are close (ZN's Aug-33s), the date is uncertain and could be either.

Duration: the DV01 over the futures settlement price, in years (the futures' modified duration).

Charts

Left: futures DV01 at each shift, with the vol ±10% band and the CTD-based staircase; below, each key rate's share of that DV01 (key rates are measured against the Treasury par curve, so low-coupon bonds load more than 100% at their own maturity and negatively at shorter tenors). Hover to read the CTD at a shift and the likeliest CTDs at delivery from there. Right: the change in each key-rate DV01 from today at the shift selected on the left: hover to move it, click to pin it, or use the slider. Markers are the CTD-based change, which jumps at each switch. Below: DV01 and effective maturity on each date, against the CTD-based figures and the first date's CTD held throughout; the gap between that constant-CTD line and the probability-weighted line is the part of the change caused by the basket. Circles mark a change of CTD, which the hover names. Drag across dates to zoom, double-click to reset.
